WHAT MAKES THIS WORK OPERATIONALLY DIFFERENT

Multi-party approval

Owner, manager, occupant or committee approval may be required before extra work proceeds.

Access coordination

Keys, occupants, building rules and common-area access can determine whether a visit succeeds.

Stakeholder reporting

The person requesting work may not be the person present at the property, so evidence and closeout reporting matter.
