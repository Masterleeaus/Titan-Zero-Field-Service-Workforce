TITAN GO

The job in the worker’s pocket.

Titan Go is the mobile Field app for workers. It shows what they are doing today and gives them the customer, site, checklist, time, materials and proof they need for each job.

WHAT WORKERS CAN DO
  • ✓
    See today’s jobs

    Know where to go, when to arrive and what is next.

  • ✓
    Open the job

    See scope, customer/site details and instructions.

  • ✓
    Use checklists

    Follow the required tasks and work instructions.

  • ✓
    Record time and materials

    Keep labour and material use against the correct job.

  • ✓
    Capture variations

    Record changed scope and customer approval.

  • ✓
    Add evidence

    Take photos, collect signatures and attach notes.

  • ✓
    Report incidents

    Escalate safety or operational problems.

  • ✓
    Complete the job

    Close only when required tasks and proof are present.

OFFLINE MODE

Keep working when mobile reception disappears.

Titan Go can keep selected job information and safe actions available offline. Notes, evidence and permitted job actions queue on the device, then recheck the job when the connection returns before syncing.
